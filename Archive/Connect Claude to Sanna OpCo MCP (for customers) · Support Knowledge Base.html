Connect Claude to Sanna OpCo MCP (for customers)

This entry needs re-verification. The steps can be out of date.
Kind
How-to
Topic
Mcp
Applies when
A customer who is a member of an organization in Sanna (opco.sanna.co) wants to use Sanna from their own Claude: read and answer questions from their accountant, ask a question, send receipts and other files, and register expenses. Send this entry to the customer. Does not apply to Sanna staff: see the entries for Sanna OpCo MCP (Staff) and Sanna OpCo MCP (Customer). Does not apply to customers on HoldCo (app.sanna.co).
Last verified
2026-11-26 by emre@sanna.co
Sources
  • Slack thread 1
  • Slack thread 2

  1. Open Claude in the browser or in the desktop app.

  2. Go to Settings → Customize → Connectors, click Add, then Add custom connector.

  3. Fill in:

    • Name: Sanna MCP
    • URL: https://opco.sanna.co/api/mcp

    Click Continue.

  4. Keep the values that Claude detected: Sign in now under Authentication, and Use Claude's published identity under OAuth client. Do not add a header. Click Add.

  5. Click Connect on the new Sanna MCP connector. Sanna opens in the browser.

  6. Log in to Sanna with the email address that has access to your company.

  7. On the page Koble Claude til Sanna?, check that the line under the title names claude.ai.

  8. The page opens with every access ticked, because Claude asks for all of them. Choose the access:

    • To let Claude answer your accountant, ask a question, send files and register expenses for you, keep all the ticks and click Godkjenn 10 tilganger.
    • To let Claude only read, click Bare lese, then Godkjenn lesetilgang.

  9. Back in Claude, try for example: "Does my accountant have any questions for me?", or attach a receipt and write "Send this receipt to Sanna".

If something goes wrong

  • Add custom connector is greyed out: your Claude account belongs to a company plan, and only the owner of that plan can add a connector. Ask the owner to add it with the values in step 3.
  • The page says Kontoen … er ikke medlem av noen organisasjon: you logged in with an email address that has no access to your company in Sanna. Log in with the address that Sanna invited, or ask your accountant for access.
  • The page says Forespørselen kan ikke behandles: close the page and click Connect in Claude again.

Remove the access

In Sanna, go to Profil → Tilgang and click Trekk tilbake on Claude. Claude loses the access at once.
